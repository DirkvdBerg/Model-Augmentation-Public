Define the actuator forces
$\uact=[F_{X1}\;F_{X2}\;F_Y]^\top$, the measured positions
$\qact=[X_1\;X_2\;Y]^\top$, and the generalized coordinates
$q=[X\;\Theta\;Y]^\top$. Let \(L_b\) denote the cross-arm length between the two flexible joints. 
The coordinate and force transformations are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  q &= P^{-\top}\qact,
  \qquad
  u = P\uact,\\
  P &=
  \begin{bmatrix}
    1 & 1 & 0\\
    L_b/2 & -L_b/2 & 0\\
    0 & 0 & 1
  \end{bmatrix},
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:Ptransform}
\end{equation}
where $u$ denotes the generalized force.

The measured payload position $Y$ is both a state and the scheduling variable,
making the baseline a self-scheduled LPV model \todo{source, or do i state it earlier..?}. 
Solving \eqref{eq:eom} for $\ddot q$ requires $M(Y)^{-1}$, so the model
is rationally dependent on $Y$. An Linear Fractional Representation (LFR) is introduced to separate this rational scheduling dependence.
We move the dependence out of the LTI part $G$ and into a Delta-block $\Dsch(Y)$, 
with the scheduling being connected in feedback with $G$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:lfr}) \cite{drenth2025lpvlfr,hoekstra2026lfr}.

To separate the scheduling variable $Y$, deconstruct 
the \kw{inertia matrix} \eqref{eq:mass_matrix} into its $Y$ dependent components,
\begin{equation}
  M(Y)=M_0+YM_1+Y^2M_2.
  \label{eq:Msplit}
\end{equation}
$M_0$, $M_1$ and $M_2$ are given in Appendix~\ref{app:lfr-details}.
Substituting \eqref{eq:Msplit} into \eqref{eq:eom} gives
\begin{equation}
  M_0\ddot q+M_1(Y\ddot q)+M_2(Y^2\ddot q)=u-C\dot q-Kq.
  \label{eq:lfr_mass_factorization}
\end{equation}
Here $Y$ only enters through $Y\ddot q$ and $Y^2\ddot q$, 
with $Y^2\ddot q=Y(Y\ddot q)$. This lets us apply the same scheduling variable twice 
instead of introducing a second scheduling variable $Y^2$,
\begin{equation}
  \ddot q\xrightarrow{\;Y\;}a_1\xrightarrow{\;Y\;}a_2,
  \label{eq:lfr_chain}
\end{equation}
with $a_1=Y\ddot q$ and $a_2=Y^2\ddot q$.

We treat $a_1$ and $a_2$ as independent signals \eqref{eq:lfr_mass_factorization}
becomes
\begin{equation}
  M_0\ddot q+M_1a_1+M_2a_2=u-C\dot q-Kq,
  \label{eq:lfr_constant}
\end{equation}
which contains no $Y$.
The \kw{latent variables} are $z=\col(\ddot q,a_1)$ and $w=\col(a_1,a_2)$,
with the \kw{Delta-block} mapping $z$ to $w$.
\begin{equation}
  w=\begin{bmatrix}a_1\\a_2\end{bmatrix}
   =\begin{bmatrix}Y\ddot q\\Ya_1\end{bmatrix}
   =\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix}YI_3&0\\0&YI_3\end{bmatrix}}_{\Dsch(Y)=YI_6}
    \begin{bmatrix}\ddot q\\a_1\end{bmatrix}
   =\Dsch(Y)z.
  \label{eq:delta}
\end{equation}
We do not claim this \kw{realization} to be \kw{minimal} \todo{I have an SVD reduction add this in the appendix or not?}.
We can write the LTI part $G$ of the LFR in the continous-time form,
\cite[Eq.~(6)]{drenth2025lpvlfr},
\begin{equation}
  \begin{bmatrix}\dot{\tilde x}\\ z\\ q\end{bmatrix}
  =G\begin{bmatrix}\tilde x\\ w\\ u\end{bmatrix},
  \label{eq:lfr_G}
\end{equation}
where $\tilde x=\col(q,\dot q)$ is the \kw{state} and $q$ the \kw{output}.
The first block row is the \kw{state equation}, with $\ddot q$ obtained from
\eqref{eq:lfr_constant} by multiplying with $M_0^{-1}$. For the blocks of $G$ Appendix~\ref{app:lfr-details}.
\\
Closing the \kw{algebraic loop} with \eqref{eq:delta} results in
$a_1=Y\ddot q$ and $a_2=Y^2\ddot q$, which turns \eqref{eq:lfr_constant} back
into \eqref{eq:eom}. The LFR is therefore an exact realization of the baseline.
The LFR is \kw{well posed} if the \kw{algebraic loop} has a unique solution for
every $Y$ \cite[Def.~1]{drenth2025lpvlfr}. 
For the gantry realization we show that this conditions holds 
when $M(Y)$ is nonsingular (Appendix~\ref{app:lfr-details}).
For positive \kw{masses} and \kw{inertias}, $M(Y)\succ0$ for every $Y\in\R$
(Appendix~\ref{app:lfr-wellposed}). Therefore the \kw{LFR} is well posed over the
whole $Y$-operating range.

\subsection{Physical parameters and identifiable combinations}\label{sec:params}
\ifdraft

The fourteen raw parameters $\theta$ of Table~\ref{tab:nominal_parameters} cannot
all be identified from input-output data. Two parameter vectors with the same
input-output behaviour satisfy
\begin{equation*}
  \bigl(M(Y)-M'(Y)\bigr)\ddot q+(C-C')\dot q+(K-K')q=0
\end{equation*}
along every trajectory. Since every $(q,\dot q,\ddot q)$ is attained, the
input-output behaviour fixes $M_0,M_1,M_2,C,K$, and conversely. The identifiable
part of $\theta$ is therefore what these matrices contain.

Up to symmetry, \eqref{eq:mass_matrix} and \eqref{eq:damping_stiffness_matrices}
have twelve nonzero entries. Three of them, $M_{0,33}$, $-M_{1,12}$ and
$M_{2,22}$, equal $m_h$, which leaves ten distinct functions of $\theta$. Their
Jacobian with respect to $(m_h,d,m_1,m_b,J_b,c_{g1},c_{g2},c_{b1},c_y,k_{b1})$ is
block triangular with determinant $m_hL_b^2/2\neq0$, so the ten are independent.
Exactly ten combinations of $\theta$ are therefore structurally identifiable
\cite[Def.~2.5]{hong2020global}, \cite[Def.~7]{ovchinnikov2021computing}.

We write them in physically readable form as
\begin{equation}
  \theta_{\mathrm{base}}=[k_{b,\Sigma},c_{g1},c_{g2},c_y,c_{b,\Sigma},
  m_h,m_\Sigma,m_\Delta,J_{\mathrm{eff}},d]^\top,
  \label{eq:phi}
\end{equation}
with $k_{b,\Sigma}=k_{b1}+k_{b2}$, $c_{b,\Sigma}=c_{b1}+c_{b2}$,
$m_\Sigma=m_1+m_2+m_b$, $m_\Delta=m_1-m_2$ and
$J_{\mathrm{eff}}=J_b+J_h+\tfrac{L_b^2}{4}(m_1+m_2)$, the gantry counterpart of
base inertial parameters \cite{gautier1990minimum}. The four lost directions are
$k_{b1}-k_{b2}$, $c_{b1}-c_{b2}$, $J_b-J_h$ and a mass transfer from the carriages
to the cross-arm, so estimates are reported in $\theta_{\mathrm{base}}$. Recovery
from the closed-loop records is examined in Section~\ref{sec:setup}.
Positive $\theta_{\mathrm{base}}$ does not imply $M(Y)\succ0$. For
$m_h,m_\Sigma,J_{\mathrm{eff}}>0$, $M(Y)\succ0$ for every $Y$ if and only if
\begin{equation}
  m_\Sigma J_{\mathrm{eff}}>\tfrac{L_b^2}{4}\,m_\Delta^2
  \label{eq:lfr_admissibility}
\end{equation}
(Appendix~\ref{app:lfr-wellposed}). Training therefore uses logarithmic
coordinates for the nine positive combinations and a $\tanh$-bounded $m_\Delta$
(Section~\ref{sec:aug_closed_loop}), so \eqref{eq:lfr_admissibility} and
well-posedness hold at every iterate.
