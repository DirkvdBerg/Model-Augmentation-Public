We use the lumped-parameter model of Fig.~\ref{fig:gantry_system} as our
physics-based baseline model of \eqref{eq:system}.
This model does not capture all dynamics of the system.
We identify these missing dynamics from $\mathcal D_N$ and augment the
baseline with learned components and additional
states~\cite{hoekstra2026lfr}.
