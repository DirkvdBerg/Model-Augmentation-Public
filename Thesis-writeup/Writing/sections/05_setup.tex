The system used to generate the data consists of the baseline model \eqref{eq:eom}
with two additions.
\add[own]{\kwtwo{Fig.~\ref{fig:gantry_absorber} shows the baseline model with
both additions.}}
The first is Coulomb friction at the actuators $X_1$, $X_2$ and $Y$, as
modelled by Garc\'ia-Herreros et al.~\cite{garcia2013model}.
We left it out of the baseline to add nonlinear unmodelled dynamics to the
system, for the augmented model to capture.

\del[own]{The friction works as a force against each actuator

For the second addition, we split the payload mass $m_h$ into
$m_a$ and $m_{h,r}=m_h-m_a$, with $m_a$ connected to $m_{h,r}$
by a spring and damper in the $Y$ direction\del[own]{
(Fig.~\mbox{\ref{fig:gantry_absorber}})}.
This adds a vibration mode which the 
baseline model is not able to represent with its states.

With the friction opposing each actuator's velocity on the input, 
\eqref{eq:eom} becomes
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:setup_truth}
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 M(Y)\ddot q+C\dot q+Kq&=P\bigl(\uact-F_{\mathrm{fr}}(P^\top\dot q)\bigr),\\
 F_{\mathrm{fr},i}(v_i)&=c_{c,i}\tanh\bigl(v_i/v_{\mathrm{fr}}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
 \label{eq:setup_fric}
\end{equation}
% FEEDBACK (Jasper, 2026-10-24): v_i defined (undefined variables; walk
%   W2, Dirk 2026-10-24).
where $v_i$ is the velocity of actuator $i\in\{X_1,X_2,Y\}$,
with the actuator velocities given by $P^\top\dot q$,
and $v_{\mathrm{fr}}$ sets the velocity scale of the smoothing.
For $\lvert v_i\rvert\gg v_{\mathrm{fr}}$, the friction
approaches the Coulomb law
$F_{\mathrm{fr},i}=c_{c,i}\operatorname{sign}(v_i)$
of~\cite{garcia2013model}.

\delmv[own]{F-C}{$v_{\mathrm{fr}}=\SetupFricVzero$~mm/s was chosen by simulating
the loop with values between 0.1 and 10~mm/s.
Higher values turn the friction into viscous damping under the multisine,
and lower values keep the standstill servo error above the noise level.}%

The added mass adds the displacement $q_a$ of $m_a$ relative to
$m_{h,r}$ along $Y$.
\add{We use the Lagrange-Euler equations of Section~\ref{sec:baseline},
adding the kinetic energy of $m_a$, the elastic energy of $k_a$
and the dissipation of $c_a$.
As in the baseline, we leave out the Coriolis and centripetal terms.}
With $q_{\mathrm e}=\begin{bmatrix}q^\top&q_a\end{bmatrix}^\top$, the
system is
\begin{equation}
 M_{\mathrm e}(Y,q_a)\,\ddot q_{\mathrm e}+C_{\mathrm e}\dot q_{\mathrm e}+K_{\mathrm e}q_{\mathrm e}
 =\begin{bmatrix}P\bigl(\uact-F_{\mathrm{fr}}(P^\top\dot q)\bigr)\\0\end{bmatrix},
 \label{eq:setup_abs}
\end{equation}
\end{subequations}
