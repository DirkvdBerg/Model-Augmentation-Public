\begin{abstract}
\add[own]{\kwtwo{A plant model can be used to evaluate a changed feedback
controller before it is tested on the machine.
For this, both predictive accuracy and physical interpretability are
required.
Physics-based models do not capture all dynamics, while refitting their
parameters or adding an unconstrained learned component can absorb
dynamics the baseline already describes.
We augment a physics-based model of a dual-drive gantry in parallel with a
neural network and added states, with a predictive-state initialisation,
trained in closed loop with the known controller.
To keep the physical parameters interpretable, we extend
orthogonal-by-construction (OBC) augmentation to a state-space baseline
that is nonlinear in its parameters.
The method is evaluated in simulation on held-out positions and unseen
motion profiles, against the baseline and a black box model.
The augmented model improves the prediction where the data excite the
missing dynamics, but the parameter estimates still depend on where
training starts.}}
\end{abstract}
