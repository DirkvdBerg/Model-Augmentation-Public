Table~\ref{tab:res_structure} compares the final model with the baseline at
the true parameters and with the black box on I1 to I6 (Aspect~1).
On the records with multisine, the final model lowers the $Y$ error from
116.6 to 6.2\,\% and halves the error on $X_1$ and $X_2$.
On the records without multisine, it is 0.2 to 0.4 points above the
baseline.
The black box is at 36 to 48\,\% with multisine and 97 to 128\,\%
without.
Fig.~\ref{fig:res_time_trace} shows record I1, where the final model has
6.3\,\% on $Y$ against 101.6\,\% for the baseline.
In the response from $F_Y$ to $Y$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:res_frf}), the final model
has a resonance at 2.11 to 2.13\,$f_b$ over $Y$, with damping ratio 0.036
to 0.080.
The system has it at 2.12\,$f_b$ with damping ratio 0.043.
The baseline has none.
Appendix~\ref{app:choices} gives the choices behind the final model on the
validation records.
With multisine, the $Y$ error is 6.4\,\% with the PS start, against
78.1\,\% with plain training and 86.5\,\% without added states.

Table~\ref{tab:res_obc} compares $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{OBC}}$ with
$\mathcal M_{\mathrm U}$, both from the detuned start (Aspect~2).
The norm of the combination errors grows from 30.0\,\% at the start to
83.1\,\% for $\mathcal M_{\mathrm U}$ and 47.7\,\% for
$\mathcal M_{\mathrm{OBC}}$.
In both models the damping combinations $c_{g1}$, $c_{g2}$ and $c_y$ move
furthest, and the mass combinations stay within 3.4\,\% of the true values.
The full models differ by at most 0.8 points.
Their physical parts differ by at most 0.9 points.
The network overlap of $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{OBC}}$ is lower than the 0.84 of
$\mathcal M_{\mathrm U}$.
The truth overlap is 0.59.
Without added states the order of the norms reverses, with 48.8\,\% for
$\mathcal M_{\mathrm U}$ against 68.4\,\% for $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{OBC}}$
(Appendix~\ref{app:choices}).
The appendix also gives the comparison from the correct start.

Fig.~\ref{fig:res_generalisation} gives the error per test record, with
each E record beside its I record (Aspect~3).
The final model is at 6.2 to 14.1\,\% on the records with multisine and
2.5 to 6.2\,\% on the others.
On every E record it stays within 2.0 points of its I record.
Without multisine it is up to 1.2 points above the baseline, most on E4 and
I6.
The black box is at 74 to 193\,\% on every record without multisine.
It is below the final model only on I2 $X_2$, with 10.2 against 11.4\,\%.
On the multisine twins, the final model is at 4.9 to 8.8\,\% on $Y$,
against 48 to 87\,\% for the baseline and 75 to 112\,\% for the black box.
