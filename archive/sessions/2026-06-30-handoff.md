## What was established this session (with evidence)

### 1. Jan's routing blows up — confirmed (diag12 T1)

`connect_block_signals(ANN, ["x","u"], ["xp"])` with `nw=nxd`:

- Reproduced 3 epochs in a row, consistently.

### 2. Root cause: 2 poles at z=1 — confirmed two ways

**Physical:** K matrix has zeros in X and Y rows (no stiffness → integrators in CT → z=1 in DT):
```
K = [0,  0,       0;
     0,  kb1+kb2, 0;
     0,  0,       0]
```

**Numerical (MATLAB):** `abs(eig(Ad))` at Ts=1/4000 s:
```
1.0000 / 1.0000 / 0.9998 / 0.9998 / 0.9993 / 0.9993
```
Script: `Matlab-scripts/compare_poles_gantry_vs_msd.m`
Output saved: `Matlab-scripts/Matlab-output/compare_poles_gantry_vs_msd.png`

Jan's MSD: min(1-|z|) ≈ 4.4e-3 (poles decay to 17% over 400 steps). Gantry: 0.

### 3. C_aug routing does not learn absorber dynamics — confirmed (diag12 T2 + T3)

**T2: trainable C_aug (10 epochs, nf=50, 8 trajectories):**
- `||C_aug||_F = 0.0000` across ALL 10 epochs (chicken-and-egg: ANN zero-init → x_aug≈0 → grad to C_aug≈0)
- `R2_linmap = [+0.080, +0.043]` flat — augmented states learn no absorber information
- val improves: 0.00056 → 0.00021

**T3: frozen C_aug=0 (same setup, C_aug literally cannot change):**
- val improves: 0.00056 → 0.00025 (nearly identical to T2)
- Proves: improvement in T2 comes entirely from encoder, not augmentation

**Main script (nf=400, 10 epochs):** C_aug grew to ||F||=0.019 (longer rollout breaks deadlock) but into X1/X2 rows, not Y (absorber). R2 for delta_a = 0.008. Still no absorber learning.

### 4. Encoder improves, augmentation contributes nothing

T3 is the cleanest proof: with C_aug frozen at 0, the ANN and augmented states cannot affect y at all, yet val still improves from 0.00056 to 0.00025. This improvement is purely the encoder learning a better physical state initialization.
