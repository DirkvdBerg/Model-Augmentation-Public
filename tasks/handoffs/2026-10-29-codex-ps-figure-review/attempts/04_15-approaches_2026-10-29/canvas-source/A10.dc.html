S1 builds
  1. The whole record: y with the free-run ŷ drifting off (real data slots in here).
  2. Zoom box at one τ; level 2 opens: the split at τ, y, ŷ from y(τ), the shaded gap, the window χτ.
  3. Zoom box at the split; level 3 opens: the state cells x̄τ|τ (highlight), fed by ψa.
  4. Residual stems eτ in level 3; dashed p from the cells draws over them; S1 minimisation appears.
S2 builds
  1. Level 1 is the window picture; bars χτ and χτ+1.
  2. Zoom box on their right ends; level 2: samples τ−1, τ, τ+1 as big stems, the bars end one stem apart.
  3. Level 3: cells x̄τ|τ (grey) and target ψa(χτ+1) (outline).
  4. gaug arc with uτ lands beside the target (highlight); S2 minimisation appears.
