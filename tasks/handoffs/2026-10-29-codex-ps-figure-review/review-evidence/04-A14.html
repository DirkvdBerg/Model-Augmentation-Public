S1 builds
  1. Inset gap eτ = y − ŷ; on the right, its stems after τ.
  2. Past χτ; ψa sets a hand on the clock: x̄τ|τ = (x̄1, x̄2) (highlight).
  3. Dashed projection from the hand tip to τ.
  4. Turning the hand traces a dashed wave p(x̄τ|τ) over the stems; S1 minimisation appears. Message: the hand angle is what the past must deliver.
S2 builds
  1. Two windows one sample apart.
  2. Grey hand x̄τ|τ; thin hand ψa(χτ+1) one tick further (ωTs = 2π·212/4000 ≈ 19°).
  3. gaug arc between the tips (highlight); S2 minimisation appears.
  4. Right: the two projections are neighbouring samples τ and τ+1 of one wave.
Answers best: what stepping a state forward looks like (a hand turning one tick), in a picture every control engineer already reads. Weakest: it presumes x̄ is exactly an oscillator pair; with other added states or the slow drift the metaphor overstates the result.
