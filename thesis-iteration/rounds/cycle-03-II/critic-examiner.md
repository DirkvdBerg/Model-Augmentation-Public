## Issues
1. (minor, D) After eq:lfr_det, four sentences follow the display: the iff consequence, the Drenth Thm. 6 contrast, M(Y) > 0 over the whole stroke, and the closed-form inverse. That is the "run of sentences appended to a display" that the rules forbid. Rule: README "Prose around the math" and skill "Equations carry the content" (at most one consequence). The rules exist but failed to prevent this.
2. (minor, F) The section lists the four lost directions and calls one of them "a transfer of mass from the X movers to the cross-arm". That direction alone changes J_eff by L_b^2/2 per unit, so it stays in the null space only together with a compensating change of J_b. The direction is really (m1, m2, mb, Jb) += (e, e, -2e, -L_b^2 e/2). As written, the claim is wrong. Rule: none requires a writer's own algebra (null directions, determinants) to be checked symbolically before it is stated. README "Derivation policy" item 2 asks only to show the load-bearing steps. Missing rule.
3. (minor, F) "Their Jacobian ... is block triangular with determinant m_h L_b^2/2". The sign depends on the order of the ten functions, which the text does not give, and with the natural order it is negative. Only |det| = m_h L_b^2/2 (or simply "nonzero, m_h L_b^2/2 in magnitude") is well defined. Rule: same missing verification rule as issue 2. Skill "Reasons and claims" (a claim no stronger than its display) does not catch it.
