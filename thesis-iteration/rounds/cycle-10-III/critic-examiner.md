Score: 7 / 10

## Verdict
An examiner reading once sees what is built (eq:aug_transition with spaces), which signals go in and out (u_act, q_act in the actuator frame, C^n with P^T), what is trained and fixed (vartheta = (xi, theta_aug, theta_enc); controller and L_b fixed), against which cost (eq:aug_loss, a full Hoekstra Eq. 22 style problem display with model, loop and encoder lines), and from which start (eq:aug_zero_init, eq:aug_enc_init, xi = 0). Roadmap, five one-job subsections and the closed-loop motivation (Beintema Cond. 1, Forssell, scoped as motivation) are at the level of Hoekstra 2026 Sec. 5. Criterion B passes. What keeps it from 8 or 9 is criterion A at the paragraph level: III-A starts with normalisation algebra before the reader has the model, and III-A, III-B and III-E end in runs of separate choice or mechanics paragraphs that read as blocks rather than one argument.

## Issues

1. MAJOR (A, B). III-A order, lines 242 to 305. The first display of the subsection is the four-line normalisation eq:aug_norm (with the conjugated f^n_base), and the parallel-versus-series choice (line 278) comes before the framework that defines what "parallel" means (line 286) and before the model display. The reader meets T_x(f_base(T_x^{-1}x + mu_x, ...) - mu_x) before knowing what the model is. Hoekstra 2026 gives the model structure (Sec. 4, Eq. 6) and only then the normalisation (Sec. 5.3, Eq. 28). Order that reads as one argument: need for states, framework and S-DP choice with the parallel reason, model display, then normalisation as the coordinates the model runs in.
   Rule cause: README "Math standard" item 4 ("Every later display writes the normalised symbol") combined with skill "Order", "Known before new", forces normalisation ahead of the first model display; no rule says the model structure may first be shown in physical units (as Hoekstra Eq. 6) with the normalised form following. Missing rule.

2. MAJOR (A, D). Tail paragraphs read as formula and reason blocks. III-A line 347 to 361 packs four unrelated choices into one paragraph (rows corrected and free-axis accumulation, output map unaugmented, no scheduling block versus Drenth, acyclic graph and well-posedness); III-B after eq:aug_residual_loop has four short paragraphs in a row (validity condition, x^c_tau = 0 consequence plus Kessels contrast, step order, controller rate). The step order and controller rate are realisation choices of the same component yet sit in separate paragraphs; the III-A paragraph's topic sentence ("acts on every state row and through no other path") does not cover the output map or the LPV-structure contrast.
   Rule cause: README "Prose around the math" (at most one consequence, everything else "into its own paragraph" or "one realisation-choices paragraph") produces exactly this stack; it failed to prevent the III-B split, and its "one paragraph per component" form lets unrelated choices be packed under one topic sentence in III-A.
