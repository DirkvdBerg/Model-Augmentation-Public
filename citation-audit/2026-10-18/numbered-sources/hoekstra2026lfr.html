00014 [PDF p.1]                                            Abstract


00015 [PDF p.1]                                            Nonlinear system identification has proven to be effective in obtaining accurate models from data for complex real-world systems.


00016 [PDF p.1]                                            In particular, recent encoder-based methods with artificial neural network state-space (ANN-SS) models have achieved state-of-the-art


00017 [PDF p.1]                                            performance on various benchmarks, using computationally efficient methods and offering consistent model estimation in the presence of


00018 [PDF p.1]                                            noisy data. However, inclusion of prior knowledge of the system can be further exploited to increase (i) estimation speed, (ii) accuracy,


00019 [PDF p.1]                                            and (iii) interpretability of the resulting models. This paper proposes a model augmentation method that incorporates prior knowledge


00020 [PDF p.1]                                            from first-principles (FP) models in a flexible manner. We introduce a novel linear-fractional-representation (LFR) model structure that


00021 [PDF p.1]                                            allows for the general representation of various augmentation structures including the ones that are commonly used in the literature,


00022 [PDF p.1]                                            and an encoder-based identification algorithm for estimating the proposed structures together with appropriate initialisation methods. The


00023 [PDF p.1]                                            performance and generalisation capabilities of the proposed method are demonstrated on the identification of a hardening mass-spring-


00024 [PDF p.1]                                            damper system in a simulation study and on the data-driven modelling of the dynamics of an F1Tenth electric car using measured data.
