00021 [PDF p.1]    This paper considers trajectory planning with given design constraints and design of a feedforward controller for single-axis


00022 [PDF p.1] motion control. A motivation is given for using fourth-order feedforward with fourth-order trajectories. An algorithm is given for


00023 [PDF p.1] calculating higher-order trajectories with bounds on all considered derivatives for point-to-point moves. It is shown that these


00024 [PDF p.1] trajectories are time-optimal in the most relevant cases. All required equations for fourth-order trajectory planning are derived.


00025 [PDF p.1] Implementation, discretization and quantization effects are considered. Simulations and hardware-in-the-loop experiments show


00026 [PDF p.1] superior effectiveness of fourth-order feedforward in comparison with lower-order feedforward.
