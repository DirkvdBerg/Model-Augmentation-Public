00018 [PDF p.1]                                                        menting physics-based input-output models with a learning component in an additive sense.


00019 [PDF p.1]                                                        The parametrization allows to jointly optimize the parameters of the physics-based model


00020 [PDF p.1]                                                        and the learning component. Unlike the commonly applied additive (parallel) augmentation


00021 [PDF p.1]                                                        structure, the proposed formulation eliminates overlap in representation of the system dynamics,


00022 [PDF p.1]                                                        thereby preserving the uniqueness of the estimated physical parameters, ultimately leading to


00023 [PDF p.1]                                                        enhanced model interpretability. By theoretical analysis, we show that, under mild conditions,


00024 [PDF p.1]                                                        the method is statistically consistent and guarantees recovery of the true physical parameters.


00025 [PDF p.1]                                                        With further analysis regarding the asymptotic covariance matrix of the identified parameters,


00026 [PDF p.1]                                                        we also prove that the proposed structure provides a clear separation between the physics-based


00027 [PDF p.1]                                                        and learning components of the augmentation structure. The effectiveness of the proposed


00028 [PDF p.1]                                                        approach is demonstrated through simulation studies, showing accurate reproduction of the


00029 [PDF p.1]                                                        data-generating dynamics without sacrificing consistent estimation of the physical parameters.
