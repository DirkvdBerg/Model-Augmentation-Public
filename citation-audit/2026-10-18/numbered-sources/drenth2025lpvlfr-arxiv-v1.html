00019 [PDF p.1]                                                         Abstract: Identifying control-friendly models of nonlinear systems remains one of the major


00020 [PDF p.1]                                                         challenges at the intersection of system identification and control. The Linear Parameter-


00021 [PDF p.1]                                                         Varying (LPV) framework offers a promising solution, but existing identification methods


00022 [PDF p.1]                                                         often rely on model structures with aﬀine scheduling dependency. Instead, this work proposes


00023 [PDF p.1]                                                         the use of LPV models with Linear Fractional Representation (LFR) admitting a rational


00024 [PDF p.1]                                                         scheduling-dependency, capable of modelling complex nonlinear systems with fewer scheduling


00025 [PDF p.1]                                                         variables compared to aﬀine models. This work introduces a direct parameterization to ensure


00026 [PDF p.1]                                                         well-posedness of rational LPV-LFR models, which by joint-estimation of an LPV plant and


00027 [PDF p.1]                                                         scheduling map, using only input-output data, is capable of modelling complex nonlinear


00028 [PDF p.1]                                                         systems. Accuracy of the proposed approach is shown on two simulation examples.
