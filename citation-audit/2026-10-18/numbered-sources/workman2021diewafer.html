00365 [PDF p.7]      The results from the initial bonding test of the new fine pitch

00368 [PDF p.7] D2W bonding of material with 2 m pad size. Although the


00369 [PDF p.7] bonder used did not have the necessary alignment accuracy to

00371 [PDF p.7] causing random added chain resistance, useful information was

00374 [PDF p.7] pad diameter, which agrees with the 80% value for a coarse pitch


00375 [PDF p.7] D2W test vehicle with 10 on 40 m pitch and a fine pitch W2W


00376 [PDF p.7] test vehicle with 1.9 on 3.8 m pitch.

00378 [PDF p.7] determined in this work is also within the range observed with


00379 [PDF p.7] the other test vehicles.
