00014 [PDF p.1]              Abstract: Identifying control-friendly models of nonlinear systems remains one of the major


00015 [PDF p.1]              challenges at the intersection of system identification and control. The Linear Parameter-


00016 [PDF p.1]              Varying (LPV) framework offers a promising solution, but existing identification methods often


00017 [PDF p.1]              rely on model structures with affine scheduling dependency. Instead, this work proposes the use


00018 [PDF p.1]              of LPV models with Linear Fractional Representation (LFR) admitting a rational scheduling-


00019 [PDF p.1]              dependency, capable of modelling complex nonlinear systems with less scheduling variables


00020 [PDF p.1]              compared to affine models. This work introduces a direct parameterization to ensure well-


00021 [PDF p.1]              posedness of rational LPV-LFR models, which by joint-estimation of an LPV plant, scheduling


00022 [PDF p.1]              map and noise process, using only input-output data, is capable of modelling nonlinear systems


00023 [PDF p.1]              under complex noise scenarios. Accuracy of the proposed approach is shown on two simulation


00024 [PDF p.1]              examples.
