0031 \subsection{Limitations and lessons}


0032 \begin{itemize}


0033 \item Closed-loop feedback improves numerical trainability but can hide plant mismatch, suppress orthogonality overlap, and reward a model that is poor in open loop


0034 \item Short windows underweight velocity DC bias on free integrators; varying encoder and rollout length is therefore a structural sensitivity test, not routine tuning


0035 \item One strengthened absorber, currently noiseless simulation, a finite neural-seed campaign, and truth-only dry friction bound claims about nonlinear, noisy, and dissipative missing dynamics


0036 \item State the final seed count and corrected-friction evidence here; do not retain development-era caveats after the final campaign changes them


0037 \item Central-difference velocities, force calibration, and the train-validation-test coverage grid bound the credibility of any real-data conclusion


0038 \item The research plan expected validated real-machine generalisation; any missing Telica result is a departure to disclose, not replace with design documentation


0039 \item Augmentation versus residual-FRF fitting remains a trade between trajectory-domain nonlinear prediction and a simpler frequency-domain account of missing dynamics


0040 \item Pointwise orthogonality is impossible for any nonzero missing force on this gantry: the parameter sensitivity of the three velocity rows has rank 3 at every reference sample (worst $\sigma_3/\sigma_1=5.7\cdot10^{-4}$, earlier absorber dataset), so $J^\top\Delta^*=0$ can only arise from cancellation over the record (Section~IV-C)


0041 \item Coulomb friction in the truth violates the recovery condition by construction: the $c_{g1}$ sensitivity is proportional to $\dot X_1$ and the friction is $-c_{c1}\operatorname{sign}(\dot X_1)$, so their inner product is $-c_{c1}\sum|\dot X_1|\neq0$ for any moving record (D-204); on T\_AF, OBC gives a unique split, not unbiased combinations


0042 \item The training loop runs $K_1$ at the model rate instead of the 20~kHz of the data, which changes the feedback force by a median of \SetupKmismatchMed\,\% on the records with multisine and at most \SetupKmismatchMotion\,\% on those without; the model sees a slightly different loop in the band of the added mass (Appendix~\ref{app:setup_checks}) (?: cause, half-sample offset in the reduction or Tustin warping, being checked for the final runs; drop this point if it is fixed)


0043 \end{itemize}
