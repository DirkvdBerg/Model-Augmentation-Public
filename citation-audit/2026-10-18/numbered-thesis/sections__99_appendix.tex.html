0053 \subsection{Explicit LPV-LFR realization}\label{app:lfr-details}


0054 In \eqref{eq:Msplit}, $M_0$ is \eqref{eq:mass_matrix} evaluated at $Y=0$, and


0055 \begin{equation*}


0056  M_1=\begin{bmatrix}0&-m_h&0\\-m_h&0&0\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad


0057  M_2=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&0\\0&m_h&0\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.


0058 \end{equation*}


0059 With $a_1$ and $a_2$ from \eqref{eq:lfr_chain} treated as independent signals, solving


0060 $M_0\ddot q+M_1a_1+M_2a_2=u-C\dot q-Kq$ for $\ddot q$ with $R=M_0^{-1}$ gives


0061 $\ddot q=R(u-Kq-C\dot q-M_1a_1-M_2a_2)$. With $\dot{\tilde x}=[\dot q^\top\;\ddot q^\top]^\top$,


0062 $z=[\ddot q^\top\;a_1^\top]^\top$, and $w=[a_1^\top\;a_2^\top]^\top$, the LTI part $G$ of


0063 \eqref{eq:lfr_G}, partitioned as in \cite[Eq.~(6)]{drenth2025lpvlfr}, is


0064 \begin{equation}


0065  \begin{bmatrix}\dot{\tilde x}\\z\\q\end{bmatrix}


0066  =


0067  \underbrace{\begin{bmatrix}


0068   A_x&B_w&B_u\\ C_z&D_{zw}&D_{zu}\\ C_y&D_{yw}&D_{yu}


0069  \end{bmatrix}}_{G}


0070  \begin{bmatrix}\tilde x\\w\\u\end{bmatrix}.


0071  \label{eq:app_G_partition}


0072 \end{equation}


0073 with blocks


0074 {\footnotesize


0075 \setlength{\arraycolsep}{2.5pt}


0076 \begin{equation}


0077 \begin{aligned}


0078  A_x&=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_3\\-RK&-RC\end{bmatrix},


0079 &B_w&=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\-RM_1&-RM_2\end{bmatrix},


0080 &B_u&=\begin{bmatrix}0\\R\end{bmatrix},\\


0081  C_z&=\begin{bmatrix}-RK&-RC\\0&0\end{bmatrix},


0082 &D_{zw}&=\begin{bmatrix}-RM_1&-RM_2\\I_3&0\end{bmatrix},


0083 &D_{zu}&=\begin{bmatrix}R\\0\end{bmatrix},\\


0084  C_y&=\begin{bmatrix}I_3&0\end{bmatrix},


0085 &D_{yw}&=0,


0086 &D_{yu}&=0.


0087 \end{aligned}


0088 \label{eq:app_lfr_blocks}


0089 \end{equation}


0090 }


0091 Here $\tilde x,z,w\in\R^6$ and $u,q\in\R^3$, so $G\in\R^{15\times15}$.


0092 The actuator frame enters only through $u=P\uact$ and $\qact=P^\top q$ in


0093 \eqref{eq:Ptransform}.


0094 


0095 Substituting $w=\Dsch(Y)z$ into the $z$ equation of


0096 \eqref{eq:app_G_partition} gives the algebraic loop equation


0097 $(I_6-D_{zw}\Dsch(Y))z=C_zx+D_{zu}u$. Written out,


0098 \begin{equation*}


0099  \begin{bmatrix}I_3+YRM_1&YRM_2\\-YI_3&I_3\end{bmatrix}


0100  \begin{bmatrix}\ddot q\\a_1\end{bmatrix}


0101  =\begin{bmatrix}R(u-Kq-C\dot q)\\0\end{bmatrix}.


0102 \end{equation*}


0103 The second block row gives $a_1=Y\ddot q$, and the first then gives


0104 $M(Y)\ddot q=u-C\dot q-Kq$, that is \eqref{eq:eom}, after multiplication by


0105 $M_0$. The condition of


0106 \cite[Def.~1]{drenth2025lpvlfr}, that $I_6-D_{zw}\Dsch(Y)$ is nonsingular, is


0107 therefore the condition that $M(Y)$ is nonsingular: the Schur complement of


0108 the lower identity block gives


0109 \begin{equation*}


0110 \begin{aligned}


0111  \det\bigl(I_6-D_{zw}\Dsch(Y)\bigr)


0112  &=\det\bigl(I_3+YRM_1+Y^2RM_2\bigr)\\


0113  &=\det M(Y)/\det M_0.


0114 \end{aligned}


0115 \end{equation*}


0116 Since $M(Y)$ is nonsingular, the loop is solved in closed form,


0117 $\ddot q=M(Y)^{-1}(u-C\dot q-Kq)$ with


0118 \begin{equation*}


0119  M(Y)^{-1}=\frac{N_0+YN_1+Y^2N_2}{d(Y)},\qquad d(Y)=\det M(Y),


0120 \end{equation*}


0121 where $N_0+YN_1+Y^2N_2=\operatorname{adj}M(Y)$ and $d(Y)$ are both quadratic


0122 in $Y$.


0123 The implementation evaluates both polynomials with Horner's scheme.

0126 \subsection{Positive definiteness of the inertia matrix}


0127 \label{app:lfr-wellposed}


0128 With $a=L_bm_\Delta/2$, elimination of the $m_h$ block and completion of the square give


0129 \begin{equation*}


0130 \begin{aligned}


0131   \frac{\det M(Y)}{m_h}


0132   &=(m_\Sigma+m_h)(J_{\mathrm{eff}}+m_hY^2)


0133     -(a-m_hY)^2\\


0134   &=m_\Sigma m_h\left(Y+\frac{a}{m_\Sigma}\right)^2\\


0135   &\quad +(m_\Sigma+m_h)


0136       \left(J_{\mathrm{eff}}-\frac{a^2}{m_\Sigma}\right).


0137 \end{aligned}


0138 \end{equation*}


0139 The $2\times2$ Schur complement has positive leading entry


0140 $m_\Sigma+m_h$. Sylvester's criterion therefore reduces positive definiteness


0141 to positivity of the completed-square expression for every $Y$. Hence, for


0142 positive $m_h$, $m_\Sigma$, and $J_{\mathrm{eff}}$, $M(Y)\succ0$ for all


0143 $Y\in\R$ if and only if \eqref{eq:lfr_admissibility} holds.


0144 For the physical parameterization, the difference between the two sides is


0145 \begin{equation*}


0146 \begin{aligned}


0147   m_\Sigma J_{\mathrm{eff}}-\frac{L_b^2}{4}m_\Delta^2


0148   ={}&m_\Sigma(J_b+J_h)\\


0149   &+\frac{L_b^2}{4}\left[4m_1m_2+m_b(m_1+m_2)\right],


0150 \end{aligned}


0151 \end{equation*}


0152 which is strictly positive for positive component masses and yaw inertias.


0153 


0154 In \eqref{eq:mdelta_map}, let


0155 $b(\theta_{\mathrm{base}})=\gamma(2/L_b)\sqrt{m_\Sigma J_{\mathrm{eff}}}$. The offset and scale


0156 are


0157 \begin{equation*}


0158  r_0=\frac{m_{\Delta,0}}{b(\theta^0_{\mathrm{base}})},\quad


0159  \eta_0=\operatorname{atanh}(r_0),\quad


0160  s=\frac{m_{\Delta,0}}{b(\theta^0_{\mathrm{base}})(1-r_0^2)}.


0161 \end{equation*}


0162 Here $\eta_0$ makes $\beta_\Delta=0$ reproduce the initial combination, and $s$


0163 gives $\partial m_\Delta/\partial\beta_\Delta|_0=m_{\Delta,0}$, matching the local


0164 relative-change scaling of the logarithmic coordinates. The construction


0165 requires $m_{\Delta,0}\neq0$; a start near zero makes $s$ small and the


0166 coordinate numerically inert. The exact inverse used for reporting is


0167 \begin{equation*}


0168  \beta_\Delta=\frac{\operatorname{atanh}(m_\Delta/b(\theta_{\mathrm{base}}))-\eta_0}{s}.


0169 \end{equation*}

0195 \subsection{PS stopping rule}\label{app:ps_stop}


0196 The unexplained fraction $\nu$ of \eqref{eq:ps2_nu} is evaluated after every


0197 $i_c$th update of $V$, with $\nu_i$ its value after update $i$.


0198 S1 ends at update


0199 \begin{equation*}


0200 \begin{aligned}


0201  i_1&=\min\bigl\{i\in\mathcal J:\ i=i_{\max}\ \text{or}\\


0202  &\qquad\bigl(i\ge i_{\min}\ \text{and}\ \nu^{\mathrm{new}}_i>(1-\delta_\nu)\,\nu^{\mathrm{old}}_i\bigr)\bigr\},\\


0203  \nu^{\mathrm{new}}_i&=\min_{j\in\mathcal W_i}\nu_j,\qquad


0204  \nu^{\mathrm{old}}_i=\min_{j\in\mathcal J_i\setminus\mathcal W_i}\nu_j,


0205 \end{aligned}


0206 \end{equation*}


0207 where $\mathcal J=\{i_c,2i_c,\dots\}$, $\mathcal J_i=\{j\in\mathcal J:j\le i\}$,


0208 $\mathcal W_i$ the last $w$ elements of $\mathcal J_i$, $\delta_\nu$ the


0209 plateau tolerance and $i_{\min}$, $i_{\max}$ the earliest and latest end of


0210 S1, with $\nu^{\mathrm{old}}_i=\infty$ while $\mathcal J_i$ has at most $w$


0211 elements.


0212 S2 runs if $\nu_{i_1}\le\nu_{\max}$ and, when $i_1>i_s$, also


0213 $\nu_{i_s}\le\nu_{\max}$, where $i_s<i_{\max}$ is a fixed early check.


0214 If $\nu_{i_s}>\nu_{\max}$, PS ends at $i_s$.


0215 The values are given in Table~\ref{tab:hyper}.

0306 The controller of every record is, per stage axis $j$,


0307 \begin{equation}


0308  K_{1,j}(s)=\kappa_j\,\frac{s+\omega_b/6}{s}\,


0309  \frac{s+\omega_b/3}{s+3\omega_b}\,\frac{10\,\omega_b}{s+10\,\omega_b},


0310  \label{eq:setup_K1}

0318 \begin{equation}

0321 \end{equation}
