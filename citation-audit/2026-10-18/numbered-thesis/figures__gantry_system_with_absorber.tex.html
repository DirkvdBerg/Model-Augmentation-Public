0010 % Conventions


0011 %   * Coordinates are in drawing units; the x/y keys of the tikzpicture set the


0012 %     unit, so the drawing scales while the labels keep the document's own font


0013 %     and size.  At 0.3mm the drawing is 1:1 with Garcia Fig. 2 (about 285pt


0014 %     wide); 0.26mm fits one IEEE column (252pt).


0015 %   * The origin is the cross arm centre of mass (the blue coordinate X).


0016 %   * Parts on the cross arm are drawn in its rotated frame: u along the beam


0017 %     (positive to the right), v perpendicular to it.  The measured coordinate


0018 %     Y points LEFT in this view, so Y = -u, and the absorber, which sits at


0019 %     +L0 in Y, is drawn to the LEFT of the payload.


0020 %   * Changing \th (yaw) or \Yp (payload position) moves every attached part,


0021 %     including its labels.  With \Yp > 0 the absorber ends up between the CoM


0022 %     and the payload, and the F_Y label then needs a manual nudge.


0023 %   * Schematic, not to scale: the absorber offset L0 and the mass sizes are


0024 %     drawn for legibility.  The absorber rides on the payload, so it may


0025 %     overhang the beam end.


0026 %   * Colours: red = measured coordinates, blue = generalized coordinates,


0027 %     grey = absorber (m_a, k_a, c_a, q_a), present only in the simulated


0028 %     plant and not in the baseline.  Theta is drawn once, at the CoM.


0029 %


0030 % \gantryabsorber: 0 draws the baseline only (Section II), 1 adds the grey


0031 % absorber (Section III).


0032 % \gantrydelta: 1 adds an arrow for the absorber displacement relative to the


0033 % payload.


0034 % ===========================================================================
