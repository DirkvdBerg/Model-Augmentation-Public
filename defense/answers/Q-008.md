# Q-008 Sampling rates (fact, Gerritsen)
**Fact.** Data: system and K_1 simulated with RK4 at f_sim = 20 kHz (E-014, E-016). Train, validation and test records all downsampled to T_s = 0.25 ms, 4 kHz (E-015; O-02). Reduction: servo error zero-phase low-passed at 1.6 kHz, force averaged per sample (E-017). Model: K_fb is K_1 by Tustin at T_s, so 4 kHz (E-018).
**Mismatch.** K_1 at 4 kHz vs recorded feedback force: median 0.5 %, max 1.0 % (E-020, E-021); phase-margin loss at most 3.8 deg (E-022).
**30 s.** "Data at 20 kHz, everything I train, validate and test at 4 kHz. The model's controller runs at 4 kHz, the data controller at 20 kHz; that costs a median 0.5 % in force."
