# Q-029 Data pipeline 20 kHz to 4 kHz (explain, Gerritsen)
**Steps.** System and K_1 simulated with RK4 at 20 kHz (E-014, E-016). Records downsampled to T_s = 0.25 ms, 4 kHz (E-015). Servo error filtered forward and backward with an eighth-order Butterworth low-pass at 1.6 kHz; force averaged over each sample (E-017).
**Why (my reading, the live thesis does not state it).** Low-pass below the 2 kHz Nyquist frequency of 4 kHz to avoid aliasing; forward-backward so no phase lag enters the position. Force averaged because the model holds the force constant over a step (ZOH, E-025): the average is the equivalent held force.
**Cost.** Model controller at 4 kHz vs data controller at 20 kHz: median 0.5 % force (E-020).
**30 s.** "Simulate at 20 kHz, low-pass the position zero-phase at 1.6 kHz, average the force per sample, keep every fifth sample. Filter against aliasing, average because the model holds the force."
