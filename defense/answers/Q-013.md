# Q-013 Well-posedness over the Y range and stability of the learned part (discussion, Toth)
**Position.** For the baseline, the LFR admissibility condition is enforced at every parameter update through the coordinates beta (E-084). For the augmented model the thesis has no final statement yet: the argument (acyclic interconnection, smooth blocks, Hoekstra Cond. 6, Cond. 8, Thm. 9) sits in a `\todo` at `03_augmentation.tex:459` (open item O-06). No stability guarantee is given for the learned part.
**Mechanism.** X and Y act as integrators, so a constant correction error accumulates in position (E-062); the feedback controller corrects this drift, which is why the model is simulated in closed loop (E-063).
**What else was possible.** Györök, Drenth, Verhoek, Péni, Schoukens and Tóth give a constraint-free parametrisation with a well-posedness guarantee and contraction-based stability of the augmentation structure (E-080).
**My view (opinion).** With free axes the open-loop model cannot be contracting, so the natural target is stability of the closed loop with the known controller. That is the next step.
