# Q-017 PINNs as an alternative (discussion, Hoekstra)
**Position.** The thesis places PINN and GP-based methods under physics-informed approaches that add physical knowledge during training (E-047), and notes these neural approaches still rely on a black box for the whole system (E-048).
**Mechanism.** A PINN adds physics as a soft penalty, so the physical laws are only approximately satisfied (E-077). Augmentation puts the physics model inside the simulation as an exact block; the network models only what is missing.
**What else was possible.** A black-box state-space model with a physics term in the loss.
**My view (opinion).** For interpretable parameters a soft physics loss is weaker: the parameters are not a block of the model, so a deviation cannot be traced to them.
**30 s.** "PINNs enforce physics softly through the loss; augmentation keeps the physics model as an exact block. For parameter interpretability I want physics in the structure, not in the loss."
**If pushed.** "A PINN might still predict better; I did not compare."
