# Q-014 Uncertainty bounds on the parameter estimates (discussion, Toth)
**Position.** The thesis gives no uncertainty bounds: every model is trained once with the same seed (E-057), and the estimates from the two starts differ, so they depend on the start (E-060).
**Mechanism.** With one seed, the start-to-start difference is the only spread; it is not a confidence interval.
**What else was possible.** The profile likelihood detects structural and practical non-identifiability and computes confidence intervals at the same time (E-083). Several seeds and random detuning give an empirical spread.
**My view (opinion).** First several seeds and random detuning directions. A profile likelihood of the ten combinations is feasible with the network fixed.
**30 s.** "No bounds: one seed and two starts only show the estimates depend on the start. Next: several seeds, then a profile-likelihood analysis of the ten combinations."
**If pushed.** "So I cannot say whether the damping deviation is significant against run-to-run spread."
