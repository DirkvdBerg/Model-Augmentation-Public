# Q-030 Generalisation only with multisine (claim, Schoukens)
**Claim.** The final model generalises to held-out positions and unseen profiles but only improves on the baseline when the multisine excites the missing dynamics (E-132). With multisine: below 8 % on all axes, baseline and black box above 65 % on Y (E-138). Without: slightly worse than the baseline (E-133).
**Mechanism.** The missing dynamics are the added-mass mode; motion profiles barely excite it (E-140), so on those records the baseline error is only a few percent of the servo error (E-139): little to gain.
**Honest part.** On operational profiles, nothing gained in this setup. Only four of eighteen training records are without multisine (E-134), which may explain the slight loss (my view).
**My view and next step (opinion).** The gain matters where the mode is excited: higher bandwidth controller, faster profiles. Next: more operational records in training, or production-compatible excitation.
**30 s.** "The model wins where the missing dynamics are excited, below 8 % against 65 % on Y. On ordinary motion there is almost nothing to win and it is slightly worse, partly because training is dominated by multisine records."
