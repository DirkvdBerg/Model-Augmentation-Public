\panel{Baseline in state-space form}

With $q=\col(X,\Theta,Y)$, scheduling signal $Y=q_3$ and physical state
$x_b=\col(q,\dot q)\in\R^{6}$, the LPV baseline
$M(Y;v)\ddot q+C(v)\dot q+K(v)q=Pu$ is written as

\begin{equation*}
\dot x_b
=
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & I\\[1mm]
-M(Y;v)^{-1}K(v) & -M(Y;v)^{-1}C(v)
\end{bmatrix}
x_b
+
\begin{bmatrix}
0\\[1mm] M(Y;v)^{-1}P
\end{bmatrix}
u ,
\qquad
y=C_o x_b+D_o u .
\end{equation*}

RK4 integration over one sample gives the discrete physical transition

\begin{equation*}
x_{b,k+1}=\fbase^{d}(v,x_{b,k},u_k),
\qquad
y_k=C_o x_{b,k}+D_o u_k ,
\qquad
v\in\R^{10}.
\end{equation*}

The free parameter vector $v$ collects the ten identifiable combinations and
enters $M$, $C$ and $K$ nonlinearly.

\panel{Additive augmentation in the state equation}

Physical states $x_{b,k}\in\R^{6}$ and learned states $x_{a,k}\in\R^{8}$ are
stacked into $\widehat x_k=\col(x_{b,k},x_{a,k})\in\R^{14}$:

\begin{equation*}
\begin{bmatrix}x_{b,k+1}\\[1.5mm] x_{a,k+1}\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}\fbase^{d}(v,x_{b,k},u_k)\\[1.5mm] 0\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}
\faug^{b}(\theta_a,\widehat x_k,u_k)\\[1.5mm]
\faug^{a}(\theta_a,\widehat x_k,u_k)
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
y_k=C_o x_{b,k}+D_o u_k .
\end{equation*}

Gy\"or\"ok adds the two components in the output equation,
$\widehat y_k=\varphi(x_k)\theta_b+f^{\ANN}_{\theta_a}(x_k)$ (Eq.~3). Here they
add in the state equation, and the learning component has no direct output
term: it reaches $y$ only through state propagation.

\panel{Separation of the two components}

With the selectors $S_b=\begin{bmatrix}I_6&0\end{bmatrix}$ and
$S_a=\begin{bmatrix}0&I_8\end{bmatrix}$, so that $S_bS_b^{\T}=I_6$ and
$S_bS_a^{\T}=0$, the baseline lifted to the full state is

\begin{equation*}
F_{\mathrm{base}}^{d}(v,\widehat x_k,u_k)
=S_b^{\T}\,\fbase^{d}\!\left(v,S_b\widehat x_k,u_k\right).
\end{equation*}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
 & reads & writes\\
\midrule
baseline $\fbase^{d}$ & $x_b$ only & physical rows only\\
learning component $\Faug$ & $x_b$ and $x_a$ & physical and learned rows\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The baseline is structurally zero on the eight learned-state rows. Those states
exist only because of the augmentation, and $v$ does not appear in their update.

\panel{Where the components can overlap}

Every baseline direction in the full state is of the form $S_b^{\T}w$ with
$w\in\R^{6}$, so

\begin{equation*}
\left(S_b^{\T}w\right)^{\!\T}\Faug
= w^{\T}S_b\Faug
= w^{\T}\faug^{b}.
\end{equation*}

The learned-state rows drop out of the inner product. Orthogonality between the
physics-based and the learning component is therefore a condition on
$\faug^{b}$ alone, while $\faug^{a}$ stays unconstrained.

This holds for a single state transition. A nonzero $x_a$ still affects
$\faug^{b}$, the propagated physical state and the output at later samples.
