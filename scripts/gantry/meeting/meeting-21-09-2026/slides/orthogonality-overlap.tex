\panel{Is the learned component orthogonal to the baseline?}

Lemma~3 asks for $\Phi^{\T}\Ftil=0$: the criterion is \emph{numerical} zero,
not ``small''. $\Ftil$ is the component as \emph{applied in the loop}, since
the projection is subtracted inside the state transition.

\begin{equation*}
\Ftil=
\begin{cases}
\Fann, & \text{no projection},\\[1mm]
\Fann-\Phi\,\thaux,\quad \Phi:=\Phiv, & \text{tangent arm},\\[1mm]
\Fann-\Phi\,\thaux,\quad \Phi:=\bigl[\,\Phiv\ \ \fbase\,\bigr], & \text{affine arm}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation*}

\textbf{Per sample, one step.} $\Phiv$ is the Jacobian of \emph{one} transition
at \emph{one} sample. $\rho$ stacks every sample of every record, but nothing is
propagated along a trajectory.

\begin{equation*}
\underbrace{\rho_{\Phi}=\frac{\bigl\|\Phiv\Phiv^{+}\Ftil\bigr\|}{\bigl\|\Ftil\bigr\|}}
           _{\text{share reproducible by }v}
\qquad\qquad
\underbrace{\rho_{f}=\frac{\bigl|\langle \fbase,\Ftil\rangle\bigr|}
              {\bigl\|\fbase\bigr\|\,\bigl\|\Ftil\bigr\|}}
           _{\text{alignment with the baseline output}}
\end{equation*}

\panel{Measured overlap, per sample}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcccc@{}}
\toprule
& \multicolumn{2}{c}{$\rho_{\Phi}$} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{$\rho_{f}$}\\
\cmidrule(lr){2-3}\cmidrule(lr){4-5}
& train & validation & train & validation\\
\midrule
no projection        & $0.87$            & $0.87$   & $1.9\cdot10^{-2}$ & $4.5\cdot10^{-2}$\\
OBC tangent (10 col) & $8\cdot10^{-8}$   & $0.33$   & $1.2\cdot10^{-2}$ & $3.5\cdot10^{-3}$\\
OBC affine (11 col)  & $2\cdot10^{-7}$   & $0.31$   & $1.4\cdot10^{-8}$ & $1.4\cdot10^{-2}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{1mm}
\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=1pt]
\item No projection: $87\%$ of the learned component is reproducible by a
      change of $v$, on both splits. Negation, measured.
\item Tangent and affine reach numerical zero on train.
\item $\thaux$ is solved on the training reference set; orthogonality holds
      only there.
\item Still $0.87\to0.31$ on held-out records, a $2.8\times$ reduction.
\end{itemize}

{\scriptsize Per sample, at each arm's own $\bar v$: $671\,944$ train and
$191\,984$ validation one-step samples. In training the basis refreshes every
epoch and $\thaux$ is re-solved every objective evaluation; here the
checkpoint's stored $\thaux$ is used, as on unseen data.}
