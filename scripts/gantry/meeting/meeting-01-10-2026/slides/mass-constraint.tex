\slide{Constraint on the mass difference $m_{\mathrm{diff}} = m_1 - m_2$}
{\large
\textbf{What}
\begin{itemize}
\item Joint estimation trains 10 identifiable combinations; 9 are positive (log), $m_{\mathrm{diff}}$ is signed
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Why}
\begin{itemize}
\item The model needs $M(Y)^{-1}$; positive combinations alone do not make $M(Y) \succ 0$
\item Exact condition (Schur complement on $m_h$, worst case over $Y$):
\[
M(Y) \succ 0 \;\; \forall Y \iff m_{\mathrm{total}}\, J_{\mathrm{eff}} > \tfrac{L_b^2}{4}\, m_{\mathrm{diff}}^2
\]
\end{itemize}

\textbf{How}
\begin{itemize}
\item Bounded parameterisation, valid for every optimiser step (no clamp, no penalty):
\[
m_{\mathrm{diff}} = \rho\,\tfrac{2}{L_b}\sqrt{m_{\mathrm{total}} J_{\mathrm{eff}}}\,\tanh(\eta_0 + s\,\xi), \qquad \rho = 0.99
\]
\item $\eta_0, s$: start and initial sensitivity unchanged; $\rho < 1$: strict inside the bound in floating point
\item Start at 1.6\,\% of the bound (35.0 kg)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Question:} does this make sense, or should the estimate stay unconstrained?
}
