      ▶   can introduce oscillations
      ▶   can cause actuator saturation

  How to solve this?

               •   gradual increase of feedforward forces (smoothing)
               •   by limiting higher derivatives (e.g. jerk)
      ▶   Here: using 3rd order setpoints
